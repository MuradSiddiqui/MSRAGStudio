Section 4.2 Fire Protection Doors
Doors along escape routes and between fire compartments must be fire-rated.
Specifically, the following doors must be fire-rated (T30 or higher):
- Doors between a stairwell and any corridor
- Doors of basement and underground garage access points
- Doors between different fire compartments on the same floor
- Doors of technical rooms housing electrical switchgear

Section 4.3 Escape Routes
Every occupied floor must have at least two independent escape routes.
Escape routes must remain free of obstacles at all times.
The maximum travel distance from any point in a room to the nearest
stairwell door must not exceed 35 metres.

Section 5.1 Corridor Widths
Corridors serving as escape routes must have a clear width of at least
1.25 metres. In buildings with more than 200 occupants per floor, the
clear width must be at least 1.50 metres. Door leaves opening into a
corridor must not reduce the required clear width when open.

Section 5.2 Windows and Ventilation
Rooms without windows may only be used as storage if a mechanical
ventilation system is installed. Smoke extraction openings in stairwells
must have a free cross-section of at least 1.0 square metre at the top
of the stairwell.
